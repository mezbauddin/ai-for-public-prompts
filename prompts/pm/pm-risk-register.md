# Turn a brain-dump into a structured risk register

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Extract

## Prompt

```text
Turn the rough list of worries below into a risk register table. Columns: Risk (phrased as "cause leads to event leads to impact"), Category, Likelihood (1 to 5), Impact (1 to 5), Score, Mitigation, Owner. Leave owner as [unassigned] where I have not named one. Do not invent risks I did not mention. Sort by score, highest first.

Worries:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

The "cause leads to event leads to impact" phrasing turns vague worries into proper risk statements you can actually mitigate. Banning invented risks keeps the register honest.

## Watch out

Likelihood and impact scores are a judgement call. Treat its numbers as a starting point and adjust them with the people who know the work.

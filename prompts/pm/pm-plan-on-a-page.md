# Turn a detailed plan into a plan on a page

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
Summarise the detailed project plan below into a plan on a page for a senior audience. UK English. Show the key phases, the three or four headline milestones with dates, the critical path in one line, and the top three risks. Cut the task-level detail. Keep every date exactly as given, do not round or shift them.
```

## Why it works

Senior readers want the shape, not the Gantt. Telling it to keep dates exactly stops the summary quietly drifting from the real plan.

## Watch out

Check that the milestones it picked are the ones that actually matter to the board, not just the nearest ones.

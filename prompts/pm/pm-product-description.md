# Write a PRINCE2 product description

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Write a PRINCE2 product description for {product}. UK English. Sections: purpose, composition, derivation (what it is based on), format and presentation, who produces it, quality criteria (specific and measurable), quality method, and who approves it. Keep the quality criteria testable, not vague. Mark anything I need to confirm with [...].
```

## Why it works

Testable quality criteria are the whole point of a product description, and they are the bit teams fudge. Forcing measurable criteria up front saves an argument at the quality review.

## Watch out

Check the quality criteria can really be tested. "User-friendly" is not a criterion, "passes WCAG 2.2 AA" is.

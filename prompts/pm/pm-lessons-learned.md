# Run a structured lessons learned review

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
From the retrospective notes below, produce a lessons learned summary. UK English. Group into: what went well (keep doing), what did not (stop or change), and surprises. For each lesson, suggest one concrete action and who would own it in future projects. Be specific, avoid platitudes like "improve communication".

Notes:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Banning platitudes like "improve communication" is what forces genuinely usable lessons. Tying each to an owner stops the review being filed and forgotten.

## Watch out

Anonymise the notes if the retro was candid. People stop being honest if they think their words will be attributed.

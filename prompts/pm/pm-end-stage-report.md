# Draft an end stage report for stage boundary

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a PRINCE2 end stage report from the notes below. UK English. Cover: stage objectives and whether they were met, products delivered, tolerances used (time, cost, scope), the business case position now, risks and issues carried forward, lessons, and a recommendation on whether to proceed to the next stage. Use the facts as given, do not invent progress.

Notes:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Reporting tolerances actually used against those agreed is the heart of stage control. Ending on a clear proceed or hold recommendation is what the board needs at a stage boundary.

## Watch out

Be honest about tolerances consumed. A rosy end stage report that hides slippage just moves the problem into the next stage.

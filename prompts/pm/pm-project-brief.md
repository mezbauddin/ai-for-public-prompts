# Draft a project brief or PID skeleton

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Draft the skeleton of a project brief for {project} in a UK trust. Sections: background, objectives, scope (in and explicitly out), key deliverables, milestones as [DATE], dependencies, assumptions, high-level risks, and success measures. UK English. Mark anything I need to supply with [...]. Keep it a structured skeleton, not waffle.
```

## Why it works

Forcing an explicit "out of scope" list is the single most useful thing a brief can do, and it is the bit people forget. The placeholders turn it into a fill-in-the-blanks job.

## Watch out

Scope is political. Get the sponsor to sign off the in and out lists before you circulate, not after.

# Build a stakeholder map and engagement plan

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
From the stakeholder list below, build an engagement plan. For each, give: their interest in the project, their likely influence (high/medium/low), their current stance (supportive/neutral/sceptical), what they need from me, and how often I should update them. UK English. Output as a table. Flag anyone who is high influence and sceptical as a priority.

Stakeholders:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Surfacing the high-influence sceptics as a named priority is exactly where a PM should spend their time. The "what they need from me" column turns a map into a plan.

## Watch out

Stances are sensitive. Keep this file off shared drives and never paste real names into the free Copilot Chat tier.

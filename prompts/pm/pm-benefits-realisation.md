# Draft a benefits realisation plan

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Help me draft a benefits realisation plan for {project}. For each benefit I list below, give: a measurable success metric, the baseline I would need, how and when I would measure it, who is accountable for delivering it, and whether it is cashable or non-cashable. UK English. Output as a table. Mark anything needing a baseline figure with [BASELINE].

Benefits:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Splitting cashable from non-cashable benefits is what finance and the board actually want to see. The [BASELINE] flags show you what you need to measure before go-live, not after.

## Watch out

A benefit with no baseline cannot be proven later. Capture the [BASELINE] figures before the project changes anything.

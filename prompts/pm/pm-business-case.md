# Draft a PRINCE2 business case outline

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Draft the outline of a PRINCE2 business case for {project} in a UK trust. Sections: executive summary, reasons, business options (including do nothing and do minimum), expected benefits, expected dis-benefits, timescale, costs, investment appraisal, and major risks. UK English. Mark every figure I must supply with [FIGURE]. Keep it a structured outline, not prose.
```

## Why it works

Forcing do nothing and do minimum as options is what makes a PRINCE2 business case credible to a board. The explicit dis-benefits section is the bit most drafts skip.

## Watch out

Do not let it populate the [FIGURE] markers. Costs and benefits are yours to source from real data, never the model.

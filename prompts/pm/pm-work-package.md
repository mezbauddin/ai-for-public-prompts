# Draft a work package to hand to a team

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Draft a PRINCE2 work package for {piece of work}. UK English. Cover: the products to be delivered with their quality criteria, the agreed tolerances (time, cost, scope), constraints, interfaces and dependencies, how progress should be reported and how often, and how the work will be approved. Mark anything I must confirm with [...]. Keep it practical, this goes to a team to act on.
```

## Why it works

Stating tolerances and reporting frequency in the work package is what lets you delegate and then manage by exception. The approval method removes the "is it done?" argument later.

## Watch out

Agree the tolerances with the team before you issue this, not after. A work package handed down without buy-in tends to bounce straight back.

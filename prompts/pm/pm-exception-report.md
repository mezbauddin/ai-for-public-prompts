# Draft a PRINCE2 exception report

**For:** Project and programme managers  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a PRINCE2 exception report for the project board. UK English. Cover: the exception (which tolerance has been or will be breached, by how much), the cause, the impact on the business case and on other stages, the options available with the pros and cons of each, my recommended option, and the lessons. Use the facts below, do not invent figures. Mark anything I still need to supply with [...].

Facts:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Laying out options with pros and cons, then a clear recommendation, is exactly what a board needs to give a fast direction. Naming which tolerance is breached keeps it true to PRINCE2.

## Watch out

The recommendation is yours to own. Sense-check that the option you are recommending is one the board can actually authorise within their delegation.

# Decode an email full of acronyms

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Rewrite the message below with every NHS or local acronym spelled out in full the first time it appears, with the acronym in brackets after. UK English. Keep the meaning and the tone. If you are not sure what an acronym means, leave it and flag it as [?].

Message:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Flagging the acronyms it is unsure of with [?] is what stops it guessing and quietly misleading you. Useful for anyone new to a trust.

## Watch out

Local acronyms vary between trusts. The [?] flags are where you will need a colleague who knows the patch.

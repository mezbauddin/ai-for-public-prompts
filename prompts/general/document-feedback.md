# Get tough but fair feedback on a document

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
Read the document below as a sceptical senior reader who is short on time. UK English. Tell me: the three weakest points, anything unclear or unsupported, and the one change that would most improve it. Be direct. Do not pad with praise.

Document:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Casting it as a sceptical, time-poor reader gets you the critique you would otherwise only find out about in the meeting. Banning padding keeps the feedback useful.

## Watch out

It cannot judge accuracy of facts it cannot check. Treat it as a reader of structure and clarity, not a fact checker.

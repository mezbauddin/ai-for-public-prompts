# Translate clinical jargon for a colleague in a different team

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Explain {clinical term or process} to a colleague in {their team, e.g. finance, IT}. One paragraph. Tell them what it is, why it matters to their work, and the one thing they would otherwise get wrong about it. UK English.
```

## Why it works

The "what they would otherwise get wrong" frame is the difference between a definition and a useful explanation.

## Watch out

If the term has a strict regulatory meaning (Caldicott, Section 251), do not let it paraphrase. Quote the source.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
An RTT pathway tracks the time from referral to treatment. It matters to finance because breaches carry a cost and affect reporting. The thing people get wrong: the clock can pause, so the raw date difference is not the waiting time.
```

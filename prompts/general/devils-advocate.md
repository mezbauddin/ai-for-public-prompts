# Pressure-test a decision before you commit

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
I am about to decide to {decision}. Play devil's advocate. UK English. Give me the strongest three arguments against, the assumption I am most likely getting wrong, what would have to be true for this to fail, and one cheaper way to test it first. Be blunt.
```

## Why it works

Asking for a cheap test first is the question that saves the most money. Naming the shaky assumption surfaces the risk you have stopped seeing.

## Watch out

It argues whatever side you ask it to. Use it to stress-test your own thinking, not to outsource the call.

# Tighten an email you have already written

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Tighten the email below. Keep the meaning. Cut filler. Cut hedging. Aim for half the length. Do not add new content. UK English. Keep my voice.

Email:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Asking it to keep your voice and not add content is what stops the output sounding like LinkedIn corporate.

## Watch out

Read the output for tone. Sometimes "tightening" removes a polite hedge that was doing diplomatic work.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Before: I was just wondering if you might possibly have a moment at some point to look over the attached when you get a chance.

After: Could you review the attached by Friday? Thanks.
```

# Turn scrappy notes into a status update

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Turn the scrappy notes below into a short status update for {audience, e.g. my manager}. UK English. Structure: what is done, what is in progress, what is blocked and what I need. Keep my facts, do not invent progress. Under 150 words. Keep my voice, no corporate filler.

Notes:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

The done / in progress / blocked structure is what managers scan for. "Do not invent progress" keeps you honest when the notes are thin.

## Watch out

Check the blocked section names a real ask. The model sometimes states a problem without the request that goes with it.

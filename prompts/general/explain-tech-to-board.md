# Explain a tech change to a non-technical board

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Explain {technical change} to a hospital board. No jargon. Three short paragraphs: What is changing. Why it matters to patient care. What you are asking the board to decide. UK English. Around 300 words.
```

## Why it works

The "what you are asking the board to decide" framing is what boards actually want. Most papers forget to ask the question.

## Watch out

Get the operations director to read it before you submit. Board readers spot vagueness instantly.

# Brief yourself for a meeting in 5 minutes

**For:** Anyone  
**Tool:** Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
I have a meeting in 30 minutes with {person or team}. Find the last 5 emails and any shared documents between us. Tell me: outstanding actions on me, outstanding actions on them, anything I promised to follow up on, and a one-line summary of where we are.
```

## Why it works

This is the prompt that justifies M365 Copilot existing. Without graph access, you cannot do it. With it, it takes 30 seconds.

## Watch out

It can miss documents shared via links rather than attachments. Glance at recent files manually if the meeting is high stakes.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
On me: send the revised budget (promised last Tuesday).
On them: confirm the room booking.
Follow up: I said I would check the leave dates.
Where we are: agreed the plan, waiting on final numbers.
```

# Draft a major incident update for users

**For:** IT teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a service status update for staff about {incident}. UK English. Calm, clear, no blame, no jargon. Cover: what is affected, what is not, what we are doing, the workaround if there is one, and when the next update will come. Keep under 120 words. Do not speculate on cause or fix time beyond what I tell you.
```

## Why it works

Telling it not to speculate on cause or fix time is what keeps you out of trouble during a live incident. The "next update" promise is what stops the service desk being flooded.

## Watch out

Get the on-call lead to approve before it goes trust-wide. Incident comms are scrutinised afterwards, so keep a copy of what you sent and when.

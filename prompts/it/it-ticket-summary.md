# Summarise a long ticket thread for handover

**For:** IT teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
Summarise the support ticket thread below for a colleague picking it up. UK English. Cover: the original problem, what has been tried and the result, the current state, and the single most useful next step. Keep it under 150 words. Do not invent steps that are not in the thread.

Ticket:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

A clean handover summary is the difference between a five-minute pickup and re-reading forty updates. Naming the single next step stops the new person retreading old ground.

## Watch out

Scrub any passwords, tokens or personal data out of the thread before pasting into the free Copilot Chat tier.

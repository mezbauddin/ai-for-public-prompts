# Decode a vendor email and draft a reply

**For:** IT teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
Read the vendor email below. UK English. Tell me in plain English: what they are actually asking or telling me, anything that commits us to cost or action, any deadline, and anything that sounds like upsell dressed up as a requirement. Then draft a short, firm reply that asks for the two or three things I would need to make a decision.
```

## Why it works

Separating a genuine requirement from upsell dressed up as one is the read most IT teams want from vendor mail. Drafting the clarifying reply in the same pass saves a round trip.

## Watch out

Do not let the draft commit to anything. Check it asks questions rather than agreeing to spend or timelines.

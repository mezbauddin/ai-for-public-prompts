# Turn an IT policy into a plain-English staff notice

**For:** IT teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Rewrite the IT policy below as a short notice for non-technical staff. UK English. Plain language. Tell them what is changing, what they need to do, by when, and who to contact if stuck. Keep any hard security requirement exact, do not soften it. Under 200 words.
```

## Why it works

Staff ignore policy PDFs but read a short notice that tells them what to actually do. Keeping the hard security requirement exact stops the plain-English version becoming wrong.

## Watch out

Check it has not relaxed a "must" into a "should". Security wording is where paraphrasing does real damage.

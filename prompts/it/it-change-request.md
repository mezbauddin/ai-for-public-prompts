# Write up a change request for CAB

**For:** IT teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a change request for the change advisory board. UK English. Cover: what is changing and why, the systems and users affected, the implementation plan in steps, the test plan, the rollback plan, the risk if we do nothing, and the proposed window. Mark anything needing a real figure or date with [...]. Be concise, CAB reads a lot of these.
```

## Why it works

A clear rollback plan and a stated "risk of doing nothing" are the two things CAB approvals hinge on. Writing them up front speeds the whole thing through.

## Watch out

The rollback plan is the one section never to fudge. If you cannot describe how to undo it, the change is not ready.

# Explain a records retention rule in plain English

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Explain the retention rule for {record type} to a team that wants to delete things to save space. UK English. Plain language. Tell them how long it must be kept, why, what counts as the start of the clock, and what to do when the period ends. Note that you are summarising and they should confirm against the Records Management Code.
```

## Why it works

Naming when the retention clock starts is the detail teams get wrong most often. The explicit "confirm against the Code" line keeps the summary honest.

## Watch out

Retention periods change. Always check the current Records Management Code of Practice rather than trusting a remembered figure.

# Draft a holding response to a SAR

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a holding response to a subject access request received today. UK GDPR conventions. Acknowledge receipt, set out the one-month timeline, ask for ID verification if not already provided, ask for any clarifications needed to narrow the scope. Polite, plain English. UK English.
```

## Why it works

The four asks in one letter are what the ICO guidance actually expects. Pulling them together saves you a copy-paste from past responses.

## Watch out

Check your trust template first. Local SAR teams often have wording they need exactly.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Thank you for your request, received today. We will respond within one month. To proceed we need to verify your identity, please send one form of photo ID. It would also help if you could tell us which records or dates you are interested in.
```

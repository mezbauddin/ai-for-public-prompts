# Draft a plain answer to a staff IG question

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
A member of staff has asked: "{question}". Draft a plain-English answer. UK English. Tell them the short answer first, then the reason, then where to go if they need a decision in writing. Do not overstate certainty. If the honest answer is "it depends", say so and name what it depends on.
```

## Why it works

Leading with the short answer respects the asker. Letting it say "it depends" stops it inventing a false black-and-white rule.

## Watch out

IG answers carry weight. If the question touches a real decision, route it through your formal advice process rather than sending the draft as gospel.

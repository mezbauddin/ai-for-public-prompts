# Plain-English explainer of sensitivity labels

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Explain Microsoft 365 sensitivity labels to a ward clerk. Three sections: What they are. What changes when you apply them. When you should stop and ask. Plain English. UK English. Around 250 words total. No jargon unless you explain it in brackets.
```

## Why it works

A ward clerk is a real reader, not a marketing persona. The "when to stop and ask" section is the bit that actually changes behaviour.

## Watch out

Your trust may have renamed the labels. Replace generic names with whatever yours uses before sharing.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
What they are
A tag you put on a document or email to say how sensitive it is.

What changes
Some labels stop a file being sent outside the trust.

When to stop and ask
If you are unsure whether something counts as confidential, ask before you send.
```

# Build a checklist before sharing data externally

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
I have been asked to share {dataset} with {organisation} for {purpose}. Build a pre-share checklist. UK English. Cover: lawful basis, minimisation, is there a data sharing agreement, how it will be transferred securely, retention and deletion, and who signs it off. Flag anything I would need legal or DPO input on with [DPO].
```

## Why it works

Turning the share into a checklist catches the steps people skip under time pressure. The [DPO] flags show exactly where to stop and ask.

## Watch out

A checklist is a prompt to think, not a sign-off. Do not share anything externally on the strength of this alone.

# Draft the narrative for a data incident report

**For:** Information governance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
From the facts below, draft the narrative section of a data incident report. Stick to what is in the facts. Do not infer cause. UK English. Around 200 words. Use a chronological structure.

Facts:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

The "do not infer cause" instruction is the difference between a clean factual narrative and one that pre-empts the investigation.

## Watch out

Incident reports are evidence. Print and keep the original prompt and output. Note them as a working draft only.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
At 09:14 an email containing a spreadsheet was sent to an external address. At 09:20 the sender noticed the error and recalled the message. At 09:35 the line manager was informed and the IG team was notified.
```

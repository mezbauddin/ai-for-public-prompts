# Build an agenda from a one-line brief

**For:** Admin teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Draft an agenda for a 60-minute meeting on {topic} with {attendees}. Include timings per item, owner per item, and a 5-minute slot at the end for actions and ownership. UK English. No more than 6 substantive items.
```

## Why it works

The cap of six items stops it producing a bloated 14-item list. Timings per item force prioritisation.

## Watch out

It does not know your politics. Sense-check who gets to own the first item.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
1. Welcome and apologies (5 min, Chair)
2. Last actions (10 min, Chair)
3. Main topic (25 min, Lead)
4. Risks (10 min, all)
5. AOB (5 min)
6. Actions and owners (5 min, Chair)
```

# Write a polite chaser without sounding annoyed

**For:** Admin teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a short, polite chaser email about {outstanding thing}. This is the {first / second / third} time of asking. UK English. Firm but friendly, no passive aggression. State what I need, by when, and offer to help if there is a blocker. Under 90 words.
```

## Why it works

Telling it which chase this is sets the firmness without you having to think about it. The offer to help keeps the relationship intact.

## Watch out

On a third chase, read the tone carefully. The model tends to stay too soft when the situation needs a gentle escalation.

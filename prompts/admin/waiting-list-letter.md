# Draft a waiting list update letter

**For:** Admin teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a letter updating a patient on their position on the {procedure} waiting list. UK English, warm but factual. Acknowledge the wait, explain what happens next, give the number to call to change contact details, and avoid promising a date. Keep under 200 words. Leave specifics as [...].
```

## Why it works

Telling it not to promise a date keeps you out of trouble. The "what happens next" framing answers the question patients actually have.

## Watch out

Check your PTL wording. Trusts often have approved phrasing for waiting list comms that has to be used exactly.

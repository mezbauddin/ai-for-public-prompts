# Personalise a standard response letter

**For:** Admin teams  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Take this template letter and personalise it for the patient details below. Keep the structure and the clinical content unchanged. Adjust only the name, salutation, appointment date, and any references to their specific situation. UK English.

Template:
{paste}

Patient details:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Templates exist for a reason. Telling it not to touch the clinical content keeps it from "improving" things that need to stay exact.

## Watch out

Spot-check the name and the date. Those are the bits that most embarrass you if they go wrong.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Dear Mrs Ahmed,

Your appointment is confirmed for Tuesday 18 March at 10:15 in the Cardiology clinic.

[the rest of the template, unchanged]
```

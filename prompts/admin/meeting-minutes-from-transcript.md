# Minutes from a Teams transcript

**For:** Admin teams  
**Tool:** Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
Produce minutes from the Teams meeting transcript {link or paste}. Sections: Attendees, Apologies, Decisions, Actions with owner and due date, Next meeting. Use UK English. Keep direct quotes only where they affect a decision.
```

## Why it works

Teams transcripts are messy. Naming the sections gives it a shape to fit the noise into.

## Watch out

Owners and dates are the two things to verify by hand. The transcript often does not contain them and Copilot will guess.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Attendees: ...
Apologies: ...
Decisions: agreed to move the clinic to Tuesdays.
Actions: update the rota (Tom, by Fri 14th); tell patients (Sara, by Mon 17th).
Next meeting: first Monday next month.
```

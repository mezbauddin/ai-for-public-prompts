# Build a differential checklist to think against

**For:** Clinicians  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
I am thinking through a presentation of {symptoms} in a {age, sex} patient. List the differentials worth keeping in mind, grouped as common, serious-must-not-miss, and rare. For each, name the one feature that would push it up or down your list. UK English. This is a thinking aid, not a diagnosis.
```

## Why it works

The must-not-miss grouping mirrors how clinicians are taught to reason. Asking for the discriminating feature turns a flat list into something you can actually think against.

## Watch out

This is a prompt for jogging your own reasoning, never a substitute for it. The model has no access to the patient in front of you.

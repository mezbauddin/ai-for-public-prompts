# Draft a referral letter to another specialty

**For:** Clinicians  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a referral letter to {specialty} from the notes below. UK English, standard NHS referral convention. Open with the reason for referral in one sentence. Include relevant history, current medication, and the specific question you want them to answer. End with your contact details as [YOUR DETAILS]. Keep under 250 words. Do not invent results or medications.
```

## Why it works

Naming the specific question you want answered is the bit most referrals miss, and it is the bit that stops the letter bouncing back. The placeholder keeps it from inventing your details.

## Watch out

Check the history it pulls through. It will sometimes promote an old, resolved problem into the active list.

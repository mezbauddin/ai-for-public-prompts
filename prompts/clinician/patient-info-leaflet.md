# Draft a plain-English patient info leaflet

**For:** Clinicians  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Write a patient information leaflet about {topic}. Reading age 11. UK English. Around 350 words. Use these sections: What this is. Why we think you need it. What it involves on the day. Common side effects. Less common but serious side effects. Who to call if you are worried. Use plain words. No medical jargon unless you explain it in brackets.
```

## Why it works

Specifying reading age 11 and naming the sections gives it the structure NHS leaflets actually use, without the corporate tone.

## Watch out

Get clinical sign-off before you give this to a patient. Treat the output as a first draft, never a final.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
What this is
A small camera test that looks at the inside of your tummy.

Why we think you need it
To find out what is causing your symptoms.

What it involves on the day
You will be asked not to eat for six hours beforehand...
```

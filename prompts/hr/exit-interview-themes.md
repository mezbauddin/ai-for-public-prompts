# Pull themes out of exit interview notes

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
From the anonymised exit interview notes below, identify the recurring themes. UK English. Group into: pay and conditions, management, workload, development, other. For each theme, give a count and one representative paraphrase, not a direct quote. Flag any single safeguarding or grievance concern separately.

Notes:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Asking for paraphrases not quotes protects anonymity in a small team. Pulling out single safeguarding concerns separately stops them being averaged away in the themes.

## Watch out

Anonymise the notes before pasting. Exit interviews are candid and easily identifying.

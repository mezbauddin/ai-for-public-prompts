# Draft an inclusive job description

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a job description for {role} at Band {x} in a UK trust. Sections: Role purpose, Key responsibilities (no more than 8 bullets), Essential skills, Desirable skills, About us (one paragraph). Avoid gendered language. Avoid jargon like "passionate" or "rockstar". UK English. Around 500 words.
```

## Why it works

The word and bullet caps stop it producing an unreadable wall. The named bans on jargon save a rewrite.

## Watch out

Run it past your EDI lead before posting. LLMs absorb the biases in their training data, which leans American.

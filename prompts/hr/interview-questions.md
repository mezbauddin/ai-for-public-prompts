# Build a competency-based interview pack

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Build an interview pack for {role}. Include: 4 competency-based questions tied to the job description below, 2 values-based questions, 1 short scenario, and a scoring rubric (1 to 4) for each. UK English. For each question, list two follow-up probes.

Job description:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Tying the questions back to the JD is the difference between a useful interview pack and a generic list of "tell me about a time" questions.

## Watch out

Have at least one panel member review the rubric before you go live. Scoring rubrics drift if nobody owns them.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Q1 (competency, organising): Tell us about a time you managed competing deadlines.
Probes: what did you drop? what would you do differently?
Rubric: 1 no example, 4 clear example with reflection.
```

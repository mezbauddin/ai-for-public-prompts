# Prepare for a difficult conversation

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Help me prepare for a difficult conversation about {issue} with a team member. UK English. Give me: a calm opening line, three things to keep the focus on behaviour not character, two likely reactions and how to respond, and a constructive way to close. This is preparation, not a script to read out.
```

## Why it works

Anticipating likely reactions is the part people skip and then get caught out by. Keeping focus on behaviour not character is the single most useful framing for these talks.

## Watch out

If the issue touches conduct, capability or anything formal, involve HR before you have the conversation, not after.

# Rewrite a policy into plain English

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Rewrite the policy below in plain English. Reading age 13. Keep all the obligations and timelines. Cut the legalese. Where there is a specific timeline (e.g. "within 14 days"), keep it exactly. UK English. Use bullet points for any list of steps.
```

## Why it works

Telling it to preserve timelines exactly is the safety check that lets you use the output without lawyering it again.

## Watch out

If the policy quotes legislation, double check the quoted phrasing has not been "smoothed".

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
If you want to raise a grievance:
- Put it in writing to your manager.
- They must meet you within 14 days.
- You can bring a colleague or union rep.
- You will get a written outcome within 14 days of the meeting.
```

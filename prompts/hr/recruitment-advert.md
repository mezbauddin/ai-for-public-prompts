# Write a recruitment advert from a job description

**For:** HR  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Write a recruitment advert from the job description below. UK English. Warm, human, honest about the role. Lead with why someone would want it, not a list of duties. Include band, hours, location, and closing date as [...]. Avoid "fast-paced" and "wear many hats". Under 250 words.

Job description:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Leading with why someone would want the job, not the duties, is what separates an advert that gets applicants from one that gets ignored. The banned cliches save a rewrite.

## Watch out

Check it has carried the essential criteria through accurately. Adverts that misstate requirements cause shortlisting headaches later.

# Plan a realistic day around fixed commitments

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Here are my director’s fixed meetings and their to-do list for {day}. Build a realistic plan for the day. UK English. Slot the to-dos into the gaps, protect at least one block for focused work and a proper lunch, and put the highest-priority task in their best energy window in the morning. Flag anything that will not fit and suggest what to move or drop.

Fixed meetings:
{paste}

To-dos:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Protecting a focus block and being honest about what will not fit is what separates a plan from a wish list. Putting the priority task in the morning respects how people actually work.

## Watch out

You know their rhythms better than the model. Adjust the energy assumptions to the person, some do their best thinking late.

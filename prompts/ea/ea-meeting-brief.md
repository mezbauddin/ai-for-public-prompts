# Build a pre-meeting brief for your exec

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
My director has a meeting with {person or group} on {date}. Pull together a one-page brief: who they are meeting and their role, the purpose, the last few emails or documents exchanged, any outstanding actions on either side, and three things my director should know walking in. UK English. Keep it to one page.
```

## Why it works

A one-page brief is what lets an exec walk into a meeting prepared in two minutes. Surfacing outstanding actions on both sides is the detail that makes them look on top of it.

## Watch out

It can miss context shared verbally or in chats it cannot see. Add anything you know that the email trail does not capture.

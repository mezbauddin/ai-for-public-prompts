# Find and untangle diary clashes

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
Look at my director's calendar for next week. List any clashes, any meetings with no agenda or no location, any day with no break longer than 30 minutes between 9 and 5, and any back-to-back meetings across different sites with no travel time. For each issue, suggest a fix. UK English.
```

## Why it works

Catching no travel time between sites and no breaks is exactly the protective work a good EA does. Asking for a suggested fix turns a list of problems into a list of decisions.

## Watch out

It does not know which meetings are immovable. Treat its suggested moves as drafts and confirm priorities before rearranging anything.

# Draft a reply in your executive’s voice

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft a reply on behalf of my director to the email below. UK English. Match this style: {warm but brief / formal / direct}. The reply should: {what you want it to do}. Sign off as [NAME]. Keep it short. Do not commit to dates or decisions I have not given you, flag those as [CONFIRM WITH DIRECTOR].
```

## Why it works

Naming the house style and flagging anything that needs the exec’s sign-off lets you draft confidently without overstepping. The [CONFIRM] markers keep you on the right side of the line.

## Watch out

Always have the director approve anything that commits them. Drafting in someone’s voice is a privilege that depends on never sending something they would not have said.

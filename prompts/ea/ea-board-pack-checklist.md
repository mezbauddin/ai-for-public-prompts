# Check a board or committee pack is complete

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Analyse

## Prompt

```text
Here is the agenda and the list of papers received for {meeting}. Cross-check them. UK English. Tell me: which agenda items have a paper, which are missing a paper, which papers arrived with no matching agenda item, and anything marked "to follow". List the chasers I need to send and to whom.

Agenda:
{paste}

Papers received:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Cross-checking agenda items against papers is fiddly and easy to get wrong by hand the night before a board. Turning the gaps into a chaser list is the part that saves the evening.

## Watch out

Confirm author names before sending chasers. The agenda does not always say who owns a missing paper.

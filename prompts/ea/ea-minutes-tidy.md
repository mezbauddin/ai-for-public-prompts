# Tidy rough minutes into a clean record

**For:** Executive support  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Tidy the rough minutes below into a clean record. UK English. Keep the house structure: present, apologies, items discussed with key points, decisions, and an action table with owner and due date. Keep it factual and neutral, cut the chat. Do not add decisions or actions that are not in my notes. Flag any action with no owner as [unassigned].
```

## Why it works

Neutral, factual minutes are a skill, and the model is good at stripping the chat while keeping the decisions. The [unassigned] flag forces the chair to fill the gaps.

## Watch out

You are the record keeper. Read every decision line carefully, because a tidied minute that subtly changes a decision causes real problems later.

# Draft a falls or pressure risk assessment narrative

**For:** Nurses and AHPs  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
From the observations below, draft the narrative for a {falls / pressure area} risk assessment. Stick to the facts given. UK English. Use the structure: current risk factors, protective factors, interventions already stated in the notes, review date as [DATE]. Do not suggest new interventions. Do not invent a score. Do not infer mobility you were not told about.
```

## Why it works

Telling it not to invent a score keeps the clinical judgement where it belongs, with you. The structure matches what most trust assessment tools expect.

## Watch out

The score and the review date are yours to set. The model drafts the prose around them, nothing more.

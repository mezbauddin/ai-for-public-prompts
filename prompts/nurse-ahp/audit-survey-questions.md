# Draft survey questions for a small ward audit

**For:** Nurses and AHPs  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Draft 10 survey questions to audit {topic} on a 24-bed ward. Mix closed (Likert 1 to 5) and open. Avoid leading questions. Group into three sections. UK English.
```

## Why it works

The mix and the leading-questions rule are what stop the survey returning a meaningless 4.7 out of 5 score.

## Watch out

Run the questions past one or two colleagues before sending. Surveys feel small but bad ones eat ward time.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Section 1, your experience:
1. How clear was the information you were given? (1 to 5)
2. What is one thing we could have explained better? (open)

Section 2, the environment... Section 3, overall...
```

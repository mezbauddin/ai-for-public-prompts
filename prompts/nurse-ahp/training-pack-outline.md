# Outline a training pack on a new procedure

**For:** Nurses and AHPs  
**Tool:** Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Outline a 45-minute training session on {topic} for a mixed audience of nurses and HCAs. Include: aims, three learning outcomes phrased as "by the end you will be able to...", a 5-minute icebreaker, the main teaching block with timings, a 10-minute practical, and three questions you would ask to check understanding.
```

## Why it works

Asking for timings and check questions forces it to think about pacing rather than just listing topics.

## Watch out

It will sometimes invent clinical detail in the practical section. Replace those bits with your own kit list and steps.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Aim: confident, safe use of the new device.
Outcomes: by the end you will be able to set it up, run a check, and spot a fault.
Icebreaker (5 min): one thing that goes wrong now.
Teaching (20 min)... Practical (10 min)... Check questions (3).
```

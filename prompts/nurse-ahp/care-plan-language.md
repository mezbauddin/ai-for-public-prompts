# Simplify a care plan for a family carer

**For:** Nurses and AHPs  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Translate

## Prompt

```text
Rewrite the care plan below for a family carer with no medical background. Reading age 11. UK English. Keep all the practical actions. Cut the jargon. Add a "what to watch for" section at the end with three things they should call the GP about.
```

## Why it works

Care plans are written for clinicians but read by families. Asking it to keep the actions but cut the jargon does the translation work without losing the safety-net content.

## Watch out

If the original plan has specific wording your service requires (legal or safeguarding), put it back in by hand. LLMs round those off.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Every morning, help with washing and dressing. Give the morning tablets with breakfast.

What to watch for, call the GP if:
- The wound looks red or hot
- A new high temperature
- Much less eating or drinking than usual
```

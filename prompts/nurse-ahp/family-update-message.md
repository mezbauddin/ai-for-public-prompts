# Draft a sensitive update message for a family

**For:** Nurses and AHPs  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Help me draft a short, warm message updating a family on {situation}. UK English. Calm and human, not clinical. Avoid false reassurance. Include one clear next step and how to reach the ward. Keep under 120 words. Leave the specifics I have not given you as [...].
```

## Why it works

Asking it to avoid false reassurance is what keeps the tone honest. The word limit forces it to be kind without rambling.

## Watch out

Read it aloud before sending. Tone is everything here, and what reads fine on screen can land wrong with a worried family.

# Write a budget variance narrative

**For:** Finance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Draft

## Prompt

```text
Write the variance commentary for the monthly finance pack. Format: one paragraph per directorate, around 80 words each. Mention pay vs non-pay split. Use the figures below verbatim. UK English. No filler. No "it is worth noting".

Figures:
{paste}
```

## Why it works

Explicitly banning filler phrases gets you commentary that reads like a person wrote it. Naming the format saves a round of rewriting.

## Watch out

Cross-check the numbers it quotes. LLMs can transpose digits when pasted figures are dense.

## Example output

Made-up data, for illustration only.

```text
Surgery is 120k overspent year to date, driven by pay (agency cover for two vacancies) rather than non-pay, which is on plan. Recruitment to both posts is expected to close the gap by Q3.
```

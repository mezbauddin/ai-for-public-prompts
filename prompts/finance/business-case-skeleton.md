# Draft the skeleton of a business case

**For:** Finance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Plan

## Prompt

```text
Draft the skeleton of a business case for {proposal} in a UK trust. Sections: problem, options appraisal (including do nothing), preferred option, costs and savings over 3 years, risks, benefits realisation. UK English. Mark every figure I need to supply with [FIGURE]. Keep it a skeleton, not prose.
```

## Why it works

The [FIGURE] markers turn the output into a checklist of what you still need to gather. Forcing a do-nothing option is what makes a business case credible.

## Watch out

Do not let it populate the numbers. Every [FIGURE] is yours to fill from real data, not the model.

# Summarise a finance pack for non-finance readers

**For:** Finance  
**Tool:** Copilot Chat  
**Task:** Summarise

## Prompt

```text
Summarise the finance pack below for a clinical director with no finance background. UK English. Three short paragraphs: where we are against budget, the two things driving the variance, and the one decision we need from them. Use the figures verbatim. No jargon, no "favourable" or "adverse" without plain wording alongside.
```

## Why it works

Asking it to translate "favourable" and "adverse" is what makes the summary land with a clinician. Naming the one decision stops it being a wall of numbers.

## Watch out

Verify every figure it quotes against the pack. Dense tables are exactly where digit transposition creeps in.
